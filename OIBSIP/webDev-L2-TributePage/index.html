01 THE STORY

FROM RAMESWARAM TO RASHTRAPATI BHAVAN

A remarkable life built on dreams.

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. Coming from a modest family, he developed an early fascination with science and a strong determination to learn.

His scientific career took him to India's space and defence programmes. He became closely associated with important national projects, including the development of indigenous launch vehicle and missile technologies.

Kalam later served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007. His approachable personality, simple lifestyle and passion for education made him especially popular among young people.

Beyond his scientific and public achievements, Kalam devoted much of his later life to speaking with students and encouraging them to pursue knowledge, creativity and service to the nation.

02 THE JOURNEY

Moments that shaped a nation.

From a young student in Rameswaram to one of India's most respected scientific and public figures.

1931

A Humble Beginning

Born on 15 October in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, into a modest family.

1954

Engineering Education

Completed his studies in aeronautical engineering at the Madras Institute of Technology.

1969

India's Space Programme

Joined ISRO and contributed to India's developing space launch vehicle programme.

1980

SLV-3 Success

The SLV-3 successfully placed the Rohini satellite into near-Earth orbit.

1998

National Scientific Leadership

Played a significant role as Scientific Adviser during India's Pokhran-II programme.

2002

People's President

Became the 11th President of India and served until 2007.

2015

A Legacy Continues

Passed away on 27 July while addressing students at IIM Shillong.

04 THE LEGACY

His greatest achievement was inspiring others.

Kalam's influence extended far beyond laboratories, government offices and scientific institutions. He believed that young people were central to India's future and spent years interacting with students, answering questions and encouraging them to think boldly.

His life continues to represent the power of education, perseverance and purposeful ambition. For millions of students, his story remains a reminder that humble beginnings do not have to limit extraordinary dreams.
