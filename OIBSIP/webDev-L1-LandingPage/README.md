# 📚 StudyNest — A Smarter Space for Students

> **Plan your study. Focus on what matters. Keep moving forward.**

## 🌱 What is StudyNest?

StudyNest is an original landing page concept designed around the everyday needs of students.

The idea is simple: studying becomes easier when tasks, goals, and progress are organized in one place.

This project presents StudyNest as a student-focused platform that can eventually grow into a complete study-planning application.

---

## ✨ What Makes StudyNest Different?

Instead of creating a generic product landing page, StudyNest was designed around a specific student experience.

The landing page includes a small **"Today's Study Plan"** dashboard that gives visitors an immediate idea of how the platform could help them organize their day.

### The concept includes:

- 📖 Organizing subjects and study material
- 🎯 Breaking larger goals into smaller tasks
- ⏱️ Planning focused study sessions
- 📊 Tracking daily progress
- 🌱 Encouraging consistent learning habits

---

## 🖥️ Main Sections

### 🏠 Hero Section

Introduces StudyNest with a clear message and call-to-action.

It also includes a sample **Today's Study Plan** showing:

- Daily progress
- Completed study work
- Current activity
- Upcoming revision

### 🧩 Features

Three core ideas are presented:

**Organize**  
Keep study subjects, notes and goals together.

**Stay Focused**  
Create focused study sessions and reduce distractions.

**Reach Your Goals**  
Turn bigger academic goals into manageable daily steps.

### 💡 About StudyNest

Explains the purpose behind the concept and how it can support students preparing for exams, assignments and new skills.

### 🚀 Call to Action

Encourages students to begin building a consistent study routine.

### 📩 Contact

Contains placeholder email and social links for the concept.

---

## 📱 Responsive Design

StudyNest is designed to work across different screen sizes.

On smaller screens:

- Navigation adapts to the available width
- Feature cards stack vertically
- The study dashboard moves below the hero content
- Text and spacing adjust for readability

---

## 🛠️ Built With

- **HTML5** — page structure
- **CSS3** — styling and layout
- **Flexbox** — responsive alignment
- **CSS Grid** — feature-card layout
- **Media Queries** — mobile responsiveness

No JavaScript is required for this landing page.
